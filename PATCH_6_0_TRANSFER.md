# V6.0 — Player walkout transfer reveal

Replaces the transfer presentation with the approved name-first walkout: first name, unquoted nickname and last name animate in, followed by the full-colour portrait, role, actual transfer route and real paid fee. Club logos appear below the names of the previous and new clubs.

Biographical names come from the existing player database (Satanic uses the approved Russian rendering Алан / Галлямов). Unknown biographies show the nickname without invented names. Free agents show FA instead of an invented former-club logo. Missing photographs retain the existing silhouette.

No accounting, transfer eligibility, player stats, save format or other animation triggers are changed. The reveal remains dismissible, supports Escape and reduced motion, and does not replay on career reload.
